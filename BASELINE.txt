Algorithmic edit targets (search space for the optimization loop):
  - Pipelining: optimistic in-flight window growth/backoff under follower lag.
  - Batching: commit-ack coalescing, MsgApp coalescing thresholds.
  - Flow control: MaxInflightMsgs / MaxInflightBytes adaptive policies.
  - Read paths: ReadIndex quorum vs. lease-based; follower read paths.
  - AsyncStorageWrites coupling: LocalAppendThread / LocalApplyThread
    pipelining when AsyncStorageWrites=true.

All edits constrained to function-scoped, single-file changes parseable by the
harness; out-of-scope edits are rejected at parse time before any test runs.
